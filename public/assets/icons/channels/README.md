# Delivery channel logos

Telegram, Discord, Zalo, Messenger and WhatsApp SVGs are from Simple Icons:
https://github.com/simple-icons/simple-icons/tree/98820a4dc8c363ca72fa2c0d294ea4a0a9bba75d/icons

Simple Icons distributes icons under CC0-1.0. Brand names and marks remain
the property of their respective owners; applicable brand guidelines still apply.
SVG paths are unchanged; fill colors match the channel palette in channel-logo.tsx.
These assets are served locally, with no external image request at runtime.

Email uses a generic inline envelope, not a provider-specific brand logo.
